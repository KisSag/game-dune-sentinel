# Dune Sentinel brief

Build a modern air-combat game over a large square desert landscape beneath a clear daytime sky. The player protects an airbase whose destruction ends the sortie. Flying near the base replenishes ammunition.

Offer three fighters with distinct handling, health and ammunition capacities. Each carries a cannon and missiles with finite ammunition. Use independent pitch, roll and yaw with throttle and braking on keyboard and mouse.

Enemy fighters engage the player. Bombers approach the airbase, attack it and depart, defending themselves with guns when the player closes in. Spawn waves every five minutes. Award 100 points per fighter and 200 per bomber, plus points for survival.

The opening menu focuses on a parked player aircraft. Display player health, score, cannon rounds and missiles during flight. Present ammunition with recognisable weapon icons. Supply a Windows build, source assets, recorded authoring operations and evaluation tasks.
